【一、文献基本信息】
================================================================================

标题 (藏文): བསམ་གཏན་མིག་སྒྲོན
Wylie转写: bsam gtan mig sgron
别名: rnal 'byor mig gi bsam gtan (禅定之禅观之眼)
英译: Lamp for the Eye in Contemplation
中文: 禅定目炬 / 禅观明灯

作者: Nubchen Sangye Yeshe (གནུབས་ཆེན་སངས་རྒྱས་ཡེ་ཤེས)
      gnubs chen sangs rgyas ye shes (努钦·桑杰耶喜)
年代: 9世纪（约842-978年"黑暗时期"前后）

馆藏编号: IOL Tib J 709 (大英图书馆·斯坦因藏品)
发现: 1908年由法国汉学家 Paul Pelliot 在敦煌莫高窟藏经洞中发现

================================================================================
【二、文献重要性】
================================================================================

bSam gtan mig sgron 是藏传佛教早期禅修传统的关键文献，具有以下重要性：

1. 大圆满 (Dzogchen) 与禅 (Zen/Chan) 的历史关系
   该文本是研究大圆满与禅宗历史关系的最重要文献之一，
   同时也揭示了作者努钦·桑杰耶喜的见解立场。

2. 四种禅修体系的分类
   努钦在此书中建立了藏传佛教禅修体系的四重分类：
   - 渐门菩萨乘 (Gradual Vehicle of Bodhisattvas / Bodhisattvayana)
   - 顿门大乘 — 对应禅/Chan/Zen宗 (Sudden Mahāyāna)
   - 密咒乘 (Mahāyoga)
   - 大圆满 (Atiyoga / rDzogs chen)

3. 保存早期藏地禅宗文献
   Carmen Meinert 的研究 "Chinese Chan and Tibetan Rdzogs Chen:
   Preliminary Remarks on Two Tibetan Dunhuang Manuscripts" 指出，
   该文献保存了早期中国禅宗与藏地大圆满之间交流的珍贵资料。

4. 真实性无可争议
   由于该写本自11或12世纪即封存于敦煌洞窟中，直到1908年才被发现，
   其真实性"超越一切质疑"(Elias Capriles)。

================================================================================
【三、努钦·桑杰耶喜 — 作者】
================================================================================

努钦·桑杰耶喜 (9世纪) 是莲花生大士 (Padmasambhava) 二十五大弟子之一。
他以在藏传佛教"黑暗时期"(约842-978 CE，朗达玛灭佛之后) 保存了大量
密宗传承而闻名。

Jacob Dalton 描述：
"Nubchen Sanggyé Yeshé is renowned for having preserved a number of
tantric lineages through the so-called 'dark period' of Tibetan history
(roughly 842-978 C.E.), when state-supported monastic Buddhism fell into
decline. Nubchen authored many works, including the Lamp for the Eye in
Contemplation (bsam gtan mig sgron), an extensive discussion of early
Tibetan contemplative systems."

努钦的其他重要著作包括《暗铠》(mun pa'i go cha, Armor Against Darkness)，
是对阿努瑜伽核心密续《意趣集经》(dgongs pa 'dus pa'i mdo) 的系统注释。

【四、Sam van Schaik 与此文献的研究】
================================================================================

Sam van Schaik 是英国藏学家，现任大英图书馆濒危档案项目负责人。
此前曾任国际敦煌项目 (International Dunhuang Project, IDP) 项目经理，
专门研究来自敦煌的藏传佛教写本。

与 bSam gtan mig sgron 及 IOL Tib J 709 直接相关的工作：

1.【敦煌藏文密宗写本编目】(2003-2005)
   Van Schaik 与 Jacob Dalton 合作编纂了大英图书馆斯坦因藏品中
   藏文密宗写本的描述目录：
   - Dalton, Jacob & Sam van Schaik. "Tibetan Tantric Manuscripts from
     Dunhuang: A Descriptive Catalogue of the Stein Collection at the
     British Library." Leiden: Brill, 2006. ISBN 9789004154223

2.【藏文写本古文字学】(2005-2008)
   研究敦煌藏文写本的古文字学，试图识别个别抄写人。

3.【Tibetan Zen: Discovering a Lost Tradition】(2015)
   Van Schaik 在此书中详细讨论了敦煌发现的藏传禅宗文献，
   bSam gtan mig sgron 作为主要参考文献。该书2019年获得
   "天竺图书奖"(Tianzhu Book Prize for Excellence in Chan Studies)。

4.【The Spirit of Zen】(2018)
   进一步探讨禅的精神传统，其中也涉及敦煌禅宗文献。

5.【Esoteric Buddhism at Dunhuang】(2010)
   与 Matthew Kapstein 合编的论文集，涵盖敦煌密教仪轨与教义。

【五、大圆满 (Atiyoga) 在 bSam gtan mig sgron 中的阐述】
================================================================================

Karmay (2007: pp. 107-108) 英译了 bSam gtan mig sgron 中关于
Atiyoga（大圆满）的关键段落：

"Now, as for expounding the doctrine of Atiyoga, the excellent vehicle,
the best and topmost yoga, the mother of all conquerors, its name is the
Great Perfection. Why? Because it gives detailed teaching with a view to
imparting direct understanding of the principle of this non-sought
spontaneity with regard to all existential elements. The sense of the
spontaneous essence, which is the innermost treasury of all vehicles and
the great 'universal grandfather' [spyi myes], is to be experienced
directly by 'self-awareness' [rang rig pas], but not as a thing to be
kept in mind. It is to be made clear to the 'self-awareness'.

How one is to know of it? In this vehicle of the high yoga, there is
nothing that can be measured by the discriminative self-intellect as
expounded in the tantras, authoritative works and precepts. Why is it so?
Because all the so-called elemental particles have never grown new feathers
or changed their colour from the beginning. It is the Buddha-nature, the
'sphere of the great circle' [thig le chen po'i klong] of the
'self-awareness'.

Who then has seen this as an object? Who has demonstrated the logic for
seeing it? To what doctrine does one entrust it? With what cognition does
one cognise it? All the elements are non-conceivable, because separately
they have no substance."

引用自：Karmay, Samten Gyaltsen (2007). The Great Perfection (rDzogs chen):
A Philosophical and Meditative Teaching of Tibetan Buddhism. Second Edition.
Boston: Brill's Tibetan Studies Library. ISBN 978-90-04-15142-0.

【六、"六相总圆" (Total Sphere in Six Aspects)】
================================================================================

bSam gtan mig sgron 中描述了"大圆"(thig le chen po) 的六个面向：

1. 法界之圆 (dbyings kyi thig le) — Sphere of the Ultimate Dimension
2. 法界清净之圆 (dbyings rnam par dag pa'i thig le) — Sphere of Purity
3. 法性之圆 (chos nyid thig le) — Sphere of Dharmata
4. 智慧明点 (ye shes thig le) — Wisdom Bindu
5. 普贤之圆 (kun tu bzang po'i thig le) — Sphere of Samantabhadra
6. 任运之圆 (lhun kyi[s] grub pa'i thig le) — Sphere of Spontaneous Presence
